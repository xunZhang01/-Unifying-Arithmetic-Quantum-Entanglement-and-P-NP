🔭 Core Thesis: Unifying Arithmetic, Quantum Entanglement, and P≠NP
Traditional complexity theory operates in a flat, discrete combinatorial space. It asks: "Can we find a shortcut?" This work asks: "What is the geometric cost of dimensional projection?"

By introducing the Cognitive Manifold, Observer Endofunctors, Arithmetic Scale Bundles, and the Boundary Obstruction Class OP/NP, this framework argues that computational complexity is intrinsically geometric. P≠NP is proven as a natural consequence of the non-vanishing topological curvature of higher-dimensional fiber bundles projected onto lower-dimensional spaces.

The Arithmetization of Quantum Entanglement (A Novel Discovery)
Beyond complexity theory, this work establishes a profound isomorphism: Quantum Entanglement can be fundamentally reduced to the algorithmic rigidity of arithmetic scale operators.

We prove that decimal multiplication (e.g., 4×2.1) is not merely a calculation, but a dimensional lifting and projection process:

Lifting (升维): Extracting the integer skeleton (A,B) and the scale exponents (m,nm,n).

High-Dimensional Operation: Pure integer multiplication (A×B) in the integer space.

Projection (降维): Applying the rigid exponent addition rule (10−(m+n)) to project back to the decimal space.

This process is mathematically isomorphic to the preparation and measurement of a quantum entangled state. Quantum non-locality is thus not a "spooky action at a distance," but a holographic projection of higher-dimensional curvature onto lower-dimensional arithmetic spaces.
